## What changed
- **`src/stores/planStore.ts`**
  - Saves to device storage: `currentPlan`, `planHistory`, `workoutLogs`, `personalRecords`, `activeWorkout` (so a workout in progress resumes after a crash), `currentWeek` and `preferredWeightUnit`.
  - Store `version: 2`. Caps raised to 1,000 logs and 500 personal records.
  - `completeWorkout` now updates the streak and weekly goal, and announces unlocked badges. The achievement code loads on demand so it stays out of the startup download.
  - Circles posting only happens when `VITE_FEATURE_CIRCLES=true`.
- **`src/stores/planStore.persistence.ts`** (new): what gets saved, and the defensive checks applied when it loads. Malformed plans, logs, records or active workouts are dropped rather than thrown. Dates are restored, and `currentWeek` is clamped to 1–4.
- **`src/stores/measurementsStore.ts`**: now saved on the device (`fitwizard-measurements`, version 1), with malformed entries dropped on load.
- **`src/lib/storage/appStorage.ts`**: `createUserDataStorage()`, the storage every user-data store should use. It is device storage plus date restoring plus a **one-time move from localStorage**: existing users' saved week and weight unit carry over, and the localStorage copy is then removed. Also, `migratingAppStorage.removeItem` now clears localStorage *before* the async step (see "New issues").
- **`src/stores/achievementStore.ts`**
  - **Weekly progress now counts finished workouts, not generated plans.** Generating a plan used to add +1, and the copy read "5 workout plans to reach your goal".
  - Weekly progress resets when a new calendar week starts (Monday, local time). It's checked on load and before each increment, using a new `weekStart` field and `rollWeekIfNeeded()`. Exports `getWeekStart()`.
- **`src/pages/Wizard.tsx`**: after a plan is generated, it counts the plan (badge check), announces new badges, and **sets the weekly goal to the plan's training days**. Previously the goal was always 5, so a 3-day plan could never be reached.
- **`src/lib/achievements/announceBadges.ts`** (new): shows a toast for each new badge. Before this, unlocks were recorded but never shown (`BadgeUnlockModal` isn't used anywhere).
- **`GoalVisualization.tsx`, `WelcomeMessage.tsx`**: the "plans" wording is now "workouts", translated via `weeklyGoal.*` keys.
- **Locales:** new `weeklyGoal.*` and `achievements.badge_unlocked` keys in **en, es, de and pt**, so the parity ratchet doesn't grow. They were added as plain text (+9 lines per file, nothing removed). See "New issues".
- **Tests:**
  - `planStore.persistence.test.ts` (new, 11 tests): restart round trip, workout resume, nothing written to localStorage, carry-over from the old version, streak and weekly goal, circles flag on/off, sanitiser cases.
  - `achievementStore.weekly.test.ts` (new, 5 tests).
  - `measurementsStore.test.ts`: rewritten as a restart round trip, plus checks that nothing goes to localStorage, plus sanitiser cases.
  - `planStore.test.ts`: the old "persists only preference fields" test was replaced by "never writes plans or workout data to localStorage". That keeps the April rule's intent (no health data in localStorage) while allowing the device storage of ADR 11.

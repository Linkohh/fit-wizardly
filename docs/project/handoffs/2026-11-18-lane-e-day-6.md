## What changed
- **`src/lib/sync/triggers.ts`** (new): `startSync(userId)` and `retrySync(userId)`.
  - Sync runs on sign-in, when the device comes back online, when the app returns to the foreground (`visibilitychange` on web; Capacitor `appStateChange` on native), and 2 seconds after the last local change.
  - Runs never overlap. Triggers that fire during a run collapse into one follow-up, so a change made mid-run is still sent.
  - Nothing runs while the device is offline.
- **First sign-in on a device:**
  - All local records are queued with `markAllCollectionsDirty()` and uploaded.
  - A toast confirms it: "Your training on this device is now backed up to your account."
  - The account id is recorded under `fitwizard-sync-first-upload`, so the next sign-in doesn't re-upload.
  - If part of the upload fails, nothing is recorded, an error toast shows, and it tries again on the next start.
  - The toast doesn't give a count. The brief's "Saved 12 workouts" needs per-table counts the engine doesn't report, and a mixed count ("12 records") reads badly.
- **`src/App.tsx`:** a new effect beside the existing sign-in sync effect (Lane E's allowed exception) lazy-loads the triggers when a user is signed in and Supabase is configured, and stops them on sign-out or an account change.
- **`src/lib/sync/outbox.ts`:** `subscribeToDirty(listener)`, which the debounce uses.
- **`src/components/sync/SyncStatusRow.tsx`** (new), shown at the top of Profile in its own card:
  - It shows Synced, Saving…, Offline, Sync problem (with **Retry**), or a **Sign in** prompt for guests.
  - It's hidden in demo mode, where there's no account to back up to.
- **Locales:** a `sync.*` namespace in en and es, added by editing the text. The de and pt gaps are recorded in `localeGaps.json`.
- **`clearLocalUserData()`** also erases `fitwizard-sync-first-upload`.
